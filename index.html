Questions?

Can I buy before finishing my profile?

Yes. An adult (18+) can order before creating or finishing a profile. Drafts stay private. Buying does not publish or activate anything. Already paid? Don’t pay again.

Do coaches need an app?

No. Tap the card with a compatible NFC phone, or open a shared profile link in a browser.

Can I update my profile after I get the card?

Yes. Your card points to your profile, so you can update your saved information without replacing the card.

Does a card guarantee recruiting opportunities?

No. SCOUTCARD is a way to present and share your information. It does not promise coach interest, offers, or scholarships.

Can a parent share the card while their child plays?

Yes. Connect the card to a published profile first. Parents or athletes can offer a tap at an appropriate moment. No coach login or app needed. Follow event and recruiting rules.

Do siblings need separate cards?

Yes. Each child needs a separate profile, card and private code. Parents can manage them in one dashboard. Always check the selected child before activation.

How does activation work?

With your physical card in hand, publish a basic profile and enter the card’s private code. Athletes use the activation page; parents use their child’s dashboard. The code is supplied with the card—not your sign-in email. Keep it off the NFC chip and out of public posts.
