GOOD QUESTIONS

make your move.

Do coaches need an app?

No. A compatible NFC phone or QR scanner opens your published profile in a browser.

Can I update my profile after I get the card?

Yes. Your card points to your profile, so you can update your saved information without replacing the card.

Does a card guarantee recruiting opportunities?

No. SCOUTCARD is a way to present and share your information. It does not promise coach interest, offers, or scholarships.

How does activation work?

Save your athlete profile, then enter the private code included with your card on the activation page. Don’t share that code publicly.
