GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Do I need to download an app?+

No. Anyone who taps or scans a TapSafe is taken straight to a secure web page.

What information is shared?+

Only the information you decide to make visible. You can change it whenever you like.

What if someone finds my TapSafe item?+

They can send you a message without ever seeing your phone number or email address.

Will there be a monthly fee?+

No. TapSafe is a one-time purchase; your profile and found-item messaging stay available without a subscription.
