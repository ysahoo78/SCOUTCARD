GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
make your move.

Can I buy before finishing my profile?

Yes. An adult can order without a finished or published profile—even before creating an account. Drafts remain private. Start with a display name and sport, keep the required age and sharing permissions, then publish when ready. Optional information can be updated later. Buying does not publish or activate anything. Already bought a card? Don’t pay again.

Do coaches need an app?

No. Tap the card with a compatible NFC phone, or open a shared profile link in a browser.

Can I update my profile after I get the card?

Yes. Your card points to your profile, so you can update your saved information without replacing the card.

Does a card guarantee recruiting opportunities?

No. SCOUTCARD is a way to present and share your information. It does not promise coach interest, offers, or scholarships.

Can a parent share the card while their child plays?

Yes. Once the card is connected to a published athlete profile, either the parent or athlete can offer a tap when a coach is available. The coach does not need your login or an app. Follow event and recruiting rules, and do not interrupt play.

Do siblings need separate cards?

Yes. Each athlete needs their own profile, physical card, and private activation code. Parents can manage multiple children in one dashboard, but should confirm the selected child before connecting a card.

How does activation work?

Build a basic profile and buy a card in either order. Once the physical card is in hand, publish the basic profile and connect the private code to the correct athlete. Parents use their dashboard’s card section; athletes use the activation page. The profile must be published to open for a coach. Keep the private code off the NFC chip and out of public posts.
