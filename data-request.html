YOUR INFORMATION

Request your data or delete it

You can ask SCOUTCARD to show you, correct, or delete information connected to your account, including private drafts, older uploaded photos and private setup-completion records. You can also ask us to remove an athlete profile or deactivate a card. We handle these requests by email so you do not need to create another account or submit sensitive details in a web form.

Send a request

  1. Email scout.card26@gmail.com with the subject “SCOUTCARD privacy request”. If the button does not open your email app, copy the address and send an ordinary email.
  2. Tell us whether you want access, correction, deletion, profile unpublishing, or card deactivation. Include the email used for your SCOUTCARD account or order and, if relevant, your public profile link or order reference.
  3. We may ask you to confirm control of the account or order before changing or disclosing information. Do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, identity documents, or private card activation codes in your first email.

If you are a parent or legal guardian, say so in the email and identify the relevant profile using its link, rather than emailing additional private information about your child. The parent dashboard supports adult-managed child profiles; children under 13 must not use the service directly. If you believe a child signed in, entered information themselves, or someone created their profile without authority, please tell us so we can prioritize reviewing it.

Parent-dashboard controls

Sign in with the managing adult’s email at the parent dashboard, then choose the child’s profile. You can review and correct the saved fields, select Unpublish immediately to stop new public requests, or use the removal section to delete that child’s profile, inbox and stored photos and disable connected cards when cleanup completes. These actions are per child; siblings’ profiles and the adult account remain separate. For a copy of your information or account-wide deletion, use the email request above. Read the parent privacy notice for limits and retention details.

What happens next

Parents can download a local JSON copy of a selected child’s saved profile from the parent dashboard. That download is not a complete account export: messages, orders and photo files require a broader request. For an unauthorized profile, unwanted messages or suspected direct use by a child under 13, see safety and reporting instructions.

We will review your request, may verify that you are authorized to make it, and reply by email. We can unpublish a profile while reviewing a deletion request. Removing data from SCOUTCARD cannot erase copies already saved by other people or search services. Some order, payment, security, or legal records may need to be retained; we will explain any limits when we respond. A request email does not instantly delete your account.

For an individual athlete account, sign in to My profile, use “Unpublish immediately” to stop new public viewing. For a parent-managed profile, use the dashboard control above. Unpublishing does not itself delete stored data.
