PRIVACY, SAFETY AND ACCOUNT HELP

Report a concern

Contact SCOUTCARD’s operators, Yash Sahoo and Toryn Ibarra, at scout.card26@gmail.com. This inbox is handled manually, not monitored continuously, and is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Unauthorized profiles or direct child use

Tell us if a profile was created without authority, someone is impersonating an athlete, a child under 13 signed in or entered information themselves, or a private activation code was exposed. Adult parents may manage child profiles; that does not authorize children to use the parent’s login.

Unwanted or suspicious messages

People sending inquiries are not verified coaches. Do not share sensitive information, follow suspicious payment requests, or feel obliged to reply. Report harassment, threats or spam, identifying the relevant profile and approximate message date. If you need to preserve evidence, keep it privately; do not repost children’s information or private messages publicly.

Open an email to report a concern

Include the relevant public profile link if you have one, a brief description, and how we can contact you.

Opening this link prepares an email; nothing is submitted until you send it from your email app. If no app opens, copy scout.card26@gmail.com into your email service. Do not send passwords, sign-in links, government IDs, full payment-card details, private activation codes, or extra identifying information about a child.

What we can do

We review reports and may seek the minimum information needed to verify authority, restrict access, unpublish a profile, disable a card, or remove unauthorized information. An email report does not automatically delete an account or decide an ownership dispute. We cannot recall copies others already made or guarantee a response time.

If you manage the profile, you can use Unpublish immediately in the parent dashboard, or turn off publishing and save in your individual profile editor. For an account-wide data copy or deletion, see privacy-request instructions.

Security issues

Report suspected exposure or account-access issues privately to the same support email with the subject “SCOUTCARD security concern.” Describe the affected feature without including other people’s data or credentials. Do not test against another customer’s account or publish exploit details in a public issue.
