We’re Yash and Toryn. Our own experiences in sports, and the athletes around us, made us want a simpler way to start a recruiting conversation.

WHY WE’RE BUILDING IT

Less searching.
More connection.

SCOUTCARD brings an athlete’s information together in one shareable profile. Instead of passing around separate links for highlights, stats, and upcoming events, you can introduce your game in one place.

The digital profile is the heart of SCOUTCARD. The NFC card is the physical way to share it — at an event, after a game, or whenever a conversation starts. Parents can carry their athlete’s card, too, so a child can focus on playing while a parent makes an introduction at an appropriate moment.

We’re building for athletes and the families supporting them, across sports. Adult parents and guardians can manage separate profiles and cards for each child, choose what to publish, and handle coach inquiries. Explore the parent dashboard →

01 / AUTHENTICITY

Your story, your voice.

Share the stats, academics, film, and experiences that matter to you. Keep your profile current as your game grows.

02 / SIMPLICITY

One clear introduction.

A tap or a shared link. Coaches can open a published profile in their browser without downloading an app.

03 / HONESTY

A tool. Not a promise.

We help you share your information. We don’t guarantee recruiting interest, scholarships, or offers. Your work is still yours.
