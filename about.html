AN INTRODUCTION.

Behind every stat is an athlete. Behind every highlight is time, effort, and a story worth sharing. We’re building a simpler way to put that story in someone’s hands.

WHY WE’RE BUILDING IT

Less searching.
More connection.

SCOUTCARD brings an athlete’s information together in one shareable profile. Instead of passing around separate links for highlights, stats, and upcoming events, you can introduce your game in one place.

The digital profile is the heart of SCOUTCARD. The NFC and QR card is the physical way to share it — at an event, after a game, or whenever a conversation starts.

We’re building for athletes across sports, with a straightforward goal: make it easier to present who you are and what you bring.

01 / AUTHENTICITY

Your story, your voice.

Share the stats, academics, film, and experiences that matter to you. Keep your profile current as your game grows.

02 / SIMPLICITY

One clear introduction.

A tap, a scan, or a link. Coaches can open a published profile in their browser without downloading an app.

03 / HONESTY

A tool. Not a promise.

We help you share your information. We don’t guarantee recruiting interest, scholarships, or offers. Your work is still yours.
