Cookies and browser storage

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Cookies and browser storage can remember information between visits. SCOUTCARD uses browser storage for the features described below. The website currently offers no optional advertising or analytics storage categories.

Sign-in session

Supabase authentication stores session information in local storage to keep you signed in. It remains available between visits until removed or replaced, such as when you sign out or clear site data. Do not share session information with anyone.

Checkout recovery

The session-storage entry scoutcard-pending-checkout remembers your order reference, email, and a fingerprint of submitted order details. It helps resume checkout and avoid duplicate order requests. Session storage normally ends with the browser tab's session; restored tabs may retain it.

Your storage choice

The entry scoutcard-storage-choice-v1 remembers that you selected essential storage only. It does not authorize advertising or analytics. You can reopen the notice using “Cookie settings” where shown or clear site data in your browser.

External services

Stripe checkout is a separate site with its own storage and privacy controls. Some SCOUTCARD pages request fonts from Google Fonts and code from esm.sh. Even without cookies, external requests share connection information with those providers. Read our privacy policy for more about service providers.

Your controls

You can clear or restrict storage in your browser settings. Doing so may sign you out or remove checkout recovery information. Our notice does not control storage on Stripe or other external websites. If optional tracking is introduced, its purpose and controls must be added before it is enabled.
