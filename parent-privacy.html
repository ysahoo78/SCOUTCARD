BEFORE YOU ENTER A CHILD’S INFORMATION

Parent privacy notice

Updated October 20, 2026

SCOUTCARD is operated by Yash Sahoo and Toryn Ibarra in Virginia, United States. For privacy help, account access, or removal requests, email scout.card26@gmail.com.

You manage the account—not your child

This dashboard is only for an adult parent or legal guardian aged 18 or older. Use your own email, enter and edit the information yourself, and handle all inquiries. Do not share your login with a child or ask a child to fill out the form. No child login is created. Individual accounts remain limited to ages 13 and older.

What we use and why

  • Your account: your email and account identifier provide sign-in and keep your family’s profiles separate from other accounts. Supabase handles authentication; Resend delivers requested sign-in emails.
  • Each child’s profile: a display name, sport and private age group (under 13 or 13–17) are required to publish, but not to create an empty private draft. Prefer a first name and last initial. Graduation year, teams, position, statistics, accolades, academics, biography, highlight link and photo are optional. These fields let you build and share the profile.
  • Your choices: we record the managing account, parent acknowledgment date/terms version and any public-sharing approval. The acknowledgment is self-reported, not verified identity, guardianship or verifiable parental consent.
  • Cards and inquiries: we connect each card to the child you select and store activation details. A message sender’s name, reply email, message and date are saved to that child’s inbox for you to manage.

Do not enter a child’s email, phone, home address, exact birth date, medical information, identity documents, or precise whereabouts. There is no child contact or event-schedule field in this dashboard. Do not add private information inside free-text fields or linked videos.

Private first. Public only if you choose.

After adult sign-in, each child you add gets a separate owner-only draft. Changes autosave after your guardian acknowledgment, so you can return on another device. You can edit a private draft without buying a card or publishing it; edits to a published profile also stay private until you publish again. Private means it is not available to public profile/NFC viewers; authorized SCOUTCARD operators and service providers can still administer the service.

Publishing is a separate, unchecked choice. When you approve public sharing and select “Publish profile,” anyone with the profile or NFC link—not only coaches—can see, copy and share all the profile content you included, including a photo or highlight link. We do not verify viewers or message senders as coaches. A card does not restrict access to its physical holder.

Your account email, the child’s private age group, acknowledgment records, inbox and private activation code are not displayed as public profile fields. If you reply to an inquiry using your email, the recipient can see your email address. No automatic coach-message email notifications are currently sent.

For parent-managed public profiles, the rendered page asks cooperating search engines not to index or follow links on it. This is not an access restriction and cannot guarantee removal from search results or prevent copying. Only keeping a profile private or unpublishing controls new public access through SCOUTCARD.

Providers, photos and external links

We use Supabase for accounts, database and private photo storage, Vercel for hosting, and Resend for sign-in email delivery. They process the information needed to provide those services, including operational logs. SCOUTCARD does not add advertising or analytics SDKs to the parent dashboard.

Optional photos are resized/re-encoded in your browser before upload; the original file and its embedded metadata are not uploaded. Published photos use image links lasting up to 5 minutes. Highlight links open external sites under their own policies; the dashboard does not embed a video player.

Buying a physical card uses Stripe separately for payment and delivery details. You do not need to buy again if you already paid in person. Order/payment records are separate from child profiles; see the full provider and order disclosures.

Parents can use “Download saved profile (JSON)” in the dashboard to save a local copy of a selected child’s saved fields, including private age and acknowledgment records. This does not include messages, photo files, orders or the entire adult account. Keep the file private; request broader records by email.

We record private first-draft and first-publication timestamps to measure setup completion, separately from adult account creation. No form contents are sent to analytics. Private drafts and older uploaded photo versions remain until removal or an authorized deletion request; there is no fixed automatic expiry. “Download saved profile” excludes the separate editing draft; ask us for a broader export.

Review, correct, unpublish or delete

In the parent dashboard, choose a child to review or correct their saved information. Use Unpublish immediately to stop new public profile requests. Use “Delete this unfinished private draft” for a child who has not yet published. To delete that child’s saved profile, use the removal section: it unpublishes first, removes stored photos through the storage service, deletes profile/inbox records, and disables connected cards when cleanup completes. If an operation fails, the dashboard explains what still needs attention; contact us for help.

Profiles and inboxes currently have no automatic expiry. They remain while you use the service unless removed by you or through an authorized request; we do not promise automatic deletion after a fixed inactivity period. Deleting a child’s profile does not delete your adult account, order/payment records, third-party copies, or provider backups. Some payment, security and legally required records may need to remain. Previously issued image links may work until their short expiry, and copies others saved cannot be recalled.

For a copy of your information, account-wide deletion, remaining uploads, an ownership dispute, or help without signing in, follow privacy request instructions or email us. We verify authority before disclosing or changing private information and explain any retention limits. Do not email passwords, identity documents or activation codes. If a child used the service directly, tell us so we can review and restrict further use.

Important limits

Email sign-in and acknowledgments do not verify adult identity or guardianship. This is an adult-operated profile workflow, not a verified-parental-consent service permitting direct use by under-13 children. A recruiting profile or physical card does not guarantee coach interest, recruitment, scholarships or admission.

Read the parent account terms, full privacy policy and browser-storage policy. This notice explains the current workflow; it does not replace any legal requirements that apply to how the service is actually used.
