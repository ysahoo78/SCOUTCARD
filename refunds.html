Refund policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Cancel before production

You can cancel your order for a full refund before production starts. Email scout.card26@gmail.com with your order reference as soon as possible. We will check the production status and confirm your cancellation. Production status at the time we receive your request determines whether this cancellation option applies.

Defective or incorrect cards

If a card arrives defective or is different from what you ordered, contact us for a replacement or refund. Describe the problem and include your order reference. If a photo helps show the issue, cover the private activation code. We will explain any return steps before you send the card back.

Unused standard-card returns

If you change your mind after delivery, contact us within 20 days of receiving your standard SCOUTCARD. We accept a return of an unused, undamaged standard card with its order reference. Contact us before mailing it so we can give you the return instructions. Once the returned card arrives and we verify its condition, we will refund the card purchase to the original payment method where possible. This does not limit your rights if the card is defective or incorrect.

Shipping delays

We expect to ship within 30 days of payment. If we cannot ship within that timeframe, we will notify you of the delay, provide a revised shipping date when available, and offer the choice to agree to the delay or cancel for a full, prompt refund of the unshipped order.

Other order problems

For a missing shipment, duplicate charge, or an order question, contact scout.card26@gmail.com. Do not send full payment-card details, passwords, or activation codes. Contact us before returning anything so we can provide the correct instructions.

Receiving a refund

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible. We will confirm when a refund has been issued. The time it takes to appear depends on your payment provider. Sending a request does not automatically issue a refund.
