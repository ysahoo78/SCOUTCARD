Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

SCOUTCARD is operated by Yash Sahoo and Toryn Ibarra in Virginia, United States. Contact scout.card26@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete your information.

Information we use

  • Accounts: your email address and account identifier let us send requested sign-in links and manage your access.
  • Athlete profiles: your name, sport, graduation year, and any position, team, school, accolades, highlight links, statistics, academics, events, and biography you enter let us create and display your profile. You may optionally upload a headshot. We resize and re-encode it before upload to reduce file size and remove embedded metadata; the original file is not uploaded. Optional fields can be left blank.
  • Messages: a sender's name, reply email, message, recipient profile, and message date let the athlete read and reply to an inquiry. Sending a message shares those details with the athlete. We do not verify that a sender is a coach.
  • Orders: we save the email and order reference you enter before checkout to match a later payment. After Stripe confirms payment, we save the shipping recipient name and address collected during checkout, payment status, and information needed to deliver your card. We also record the order's terms version and the time you acknowledge the terms and adult-purchaser requirement. Older unpaid orders may still include details collected under the previous order form.
  • Cards: activation information, public card identifiers, connected profiles, and activation dates let us connect a card to its owner.
  • Technical information: our service providers process connection and operational information, such as IP addresses, browser information, and request logs, to deliver and secure their services.

Please do not put home addresses, medical information, identification numbers, passwords, or private schedules in your athlete profile. Enter a delivery address only during Stripe checkout. Keep card activation codes private.

What becomes public

When you select the publishing option and save, your athlete profile is accessible to anyone who obtains its link, including through an NFC tap or a shared profile link. Viewers may copy or share it. Information you put in profile fields becomes public when you publish, including any contact details you type yourself.

Your account email, shipping address, private inbox, and activation code are not displayed as public profile fields. Coach messages are available to the receiving athlete and the service operators who administer the service.

To stop public viewing through SCOUTCARD, uncheck the publishing option and save. You can edit your profile at any time while signed in. Unpublishing cannot remove copies already retained by other people or services.

Headshots: photos are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket. Your saved photo can be viewed by you while signed in and through your published profile. Viewers receive short-lived image links that last up to 5 minutes; an already issued link may continue working until it expires after you unpublish. To replace a photo or return to initials, use the headshot controls and save your profile. Old photos are deleted after a successful replacement/removal when storage cleanup succeeds; interrupted uploads or failed cleanup may require administrative deletion. Contact us to request removal of any remaining stored copies.

Service providers and external sites

We use Supabase for authentication, database storage, and optional headshot file storage, Vercel for hosting and server functions, Resend for requested sign-in email delivery, and Stripe for checkout and payment processing. These providers process information needed to perform those services. Sign-in emails contain account-access links; keep them private. Child profile details are not included in sign-in emails.

Stripe collects payment details and the required U.S. shipping name and address on its checkout page. A phone number is not required for a card purchase; Stripe may offer an optional phone field for its Link faster-checkout service. SCOUTCARD's order form does not collect your full payment-card number, phone number, or delivery address. We pass an order reference and your email to Stripe to connect checkout to your order. Information necessary to deliver a physical order may be shared with the printing or delivery provider fulfilling it.

SCOUTCARD serves its website fonts and bundled application library from its own hosting. Account and profile features connect to Supabase to provide the service. The privacy page itself uses system fonts and does not load external scripts.

Highlight links, social-media links, and college-athletics links take you to external websites. Those sites handle information under their own policies. Service providers may process information outside your state or country.

Browser storage

Sign-in uses browser storage to maintain your session. Checkout uses session storage for an order reference, email, and a fingerprint of the email to help resume checkout and prevent duplicate order creation during retries. We also store your essential-storage notice choice in local storage so the notice does not appear on every visit. Clearing site storage may interrupt sign-in or checkout recovery and reset that choice. Signing out ends your session in that browser. See our cookie and browser-storage policy for details.

Before account entry, we ask you to choose an age group and, for teens, acknowledge parent or guardian permission. We retain only the self-reported age group, permission acknowledgment, and an expiry time in local storage under scoutcard-age-group-v2 for up to 30 days on that device. We do not collect an exact age or date of birth through this screen. You can use “Change age group” on the account or activation page, or clear site data, to reset the choice. This is not identity or parental verification.

The parent dashboard remembers an adult-use acknowledgment and expiration time under scoutcard-parent-entry-v1 for up to 30 days. No child profile drafts or activation codes are saved by this choice. Signing out from the parent dashboard clears it.

Retention and deletion requests

Saved profiles, messages, and order records do not currently have an automatic expiration schedule in SCOUTCARD. Records remain stored until removed through administration or a deletion request, subject to records that need to be retained for payment disputes, order fulfillment, security, or legal obligations. Provider logs and backups follow their own retention settings; deletion from the active service may not immediately remove backup copies.

To request access, correction, or deletion, follow our privacy and deletion request instructions or email scout.card26@gmail.com with the account email and what you want us to do. We may confirm control of the account before processing the request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or activation codes. If information must be retained, we will explain the reason when responding. Sending a request does not itself instantly delete your account.

Children, teens, and parent-managed profiles

Before creating a child profile, read the parent privacy notice for a plain-language explanation of the adult-only workflow, information used, public-sharing choice, service providers and removal controls.

Individual athlete accounts are for ages 13 and older. Teens aged 13–17 must involve a parent or guardian and have their permission before submitting information or publishing a profile. Children under 13 must not sign in, enter information, or use a parent’s login.

Adult parent / guardian dashboard: an adult parent or legal guardian may use their own email to create and manage separate profiles for their children, including under-13 children. The adult—not the child—must submit and edit the information and manage messages. We do not create a child login or request a child email, birth date, phone, home address, or event schedule in this workflow. We store the child’s broad age band privately, the managing adult’s account identifier, their acknowledgment date and policy version, and any approval to publish. A display name and sport are required; other profile details and a photo are optional. Prefer a first name and last initial.

Parent-managed profiles start private. A parent must explicitly approve publishing; a published profile and any included photo or highlight link can then be viewed, copied, and shared by anyone with the URL or NFC link. The parent’s account email and private age/acknowledgment records are not public profile fields. Messages are handled in the managing adult’s inbox. The parent can edit, unpublish, or delete a child profile from the dashboard. Deletion removes its active profile, inbox and stored photos when cleanup succeeds, and disables its cards. Previously issued image URLs may last up to 5 minutes, others may have saved copies, and payment records/backups have separate retention. Contact us if deletion does not complete or you need account-wide removal.

Adult and guardianship acknowledgments are self-reported, not identity or guardianship verification. Email sign-in verifies access to the mailbox; this is not a verified parental-consent service for collecting information directly from under-13 children. Parents or guardians who have concerns about a child's information, or believe a child used the service directly, should contact scout.card26@gmail.com so we can investigate and arrange removal.

Messages from SCOUTCARD

Sign-in emails are sent when requested to access an account. The current website does not offer a marketing email subscription or automatic coach-message email notifications.

Policy changes
